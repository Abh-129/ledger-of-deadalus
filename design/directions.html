The Labyrinth of Daedalus

One first-person walk through a Minoan Labyrinth that Daedalus built and cannot leave. Six chambers, one fresco and one hero object each, Ariadne's thread on the floor, the Ledger keeping score. The feeling to create: quiet dread in a beautiful room. Graded on a laptop in Chrome or Safari.

Ochre only for what was solved (the thread, Solved lines) Red only for what it cost (Cost lines, the Minotaur's chamber) Frescoes are painted without ochre or red, so the two Ledger colors keep their meaning

Recommendation: C, Moonlit Lapis, with A's plaster texture on the fresco walls. In a cold blue world the thread and the Ledger are the only warm things you see, so the ochre and red rule becomes something you feel walking, and fog does the work of hiding low-poly geometry. Reasons in full at the end.

Six inventions. Every one of them worked.

BeginSound onSources
  • Materials

    One shared MeshStandardMaterial for plaster with a single 512 px tiling noise map as roughness and bump. Each fresco is one CanvasTexture painted once at load (or a CC0 image) at 2048 x 1024, sRGB, no mipmap churn.

  • Lighting

    Two flickering PointLights per chamber, only the current chamber's lights enabled (cap four on screen). HemisphereLight in night blue as fill. One 512 px shadow from the nearest torch, everything else unshadowed.

  • Post

    ACES tone mapping, Bloom with a high threshold so only flames and the thread bloom, Vignette, faint Noise. Four point lights is the most expensive option of the three on Safari.

WatchTorchlight is warm, so the room fights the ochre rule. The flame must stay pale cream, and realism invites comparison with games that have baked lighting. Hardest of the three to make look finished.

WatchBlack-figure is Greek pottery, centuries after Minoan fresco, which the decisions ledger names as the look. It reads as stylish but less like the brief's Knossos, and flat lighting makes walking feel less like being in a place.

The Labyrinth
of Daedalus

Six inventions. Every one of them worked.

BeginSound onSources
  • Materials

    MeshStandardMaterial, lapis-tinted plaster with the same 512 px noise map as A. Thread as a TubeGeometry with an emissive ochre MeshBasicMaterial. Fresco CanvasTextures painted in limestone, lapis, olive and black only.

  • Lighting

    One cool DirectionalLight per chamber as the moon, one 1024 px shadow map, HemisphereLight fill in deep sea. Shafts are additive planes with a gradient alpha texture, depthWrite off, slow UV drift. No point lights except the red one in chamber III.

  • Post

    FogExp2 in deep sea hides draw distance and low poly. Bloom tuned so the thread, the moon and the shafts glow; Vignette and Noise. Sits between A and B in cost.

WatchBlue night can turn to murk on a dim laptop. Keep a floor on exposure and keep frescoes lit to at least moonlight level so every wall painting reads in one glance.

Recommendation

Build C, with A's plaster on the walls

  • It makes the locked colour rule physical. Everything is cold, so the ochre thread pulls you forward and the red Cost lines land like a wound. In A the torches compete with the ochre.
  • It is the closest to the brief's fresco preference that still survives no baked lighting: fog, one moon light and soft shafts flatter low-poly rooms, which A cannot do.
  • Each chamber can change only its light colour and fresco while sharing one material set: moonlit stone for I, II, IV, V; red point light for III, the Minotaur's empty chamber; a cold dawn in VI on the beach.
  • Borrow from B the hard-edged black outline on fresco figures and the meander band, so frescoes read instantly at laptop size.
